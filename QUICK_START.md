# 🚀 CalorieWise Quick Start Guide

Welcome to CalorieWise! This guide will help you get started with tracking your nutrition and building healthy habits.

---

## 📸 1. Log Your First Meal

### How to Scan Food:
1. Tap the **Camera** icon in the bottom navigation
2. Point your camera at your meal
3. Take a clear, well-lit photo showing all food items
4. Wait for AI analysis (10-15 seconds)
5. Review the nutrition breakdown
6. Tap **Save** to log the meal

### Tips for Best Results:
- ✅ Good lighting (natural light is best)
- ✅ Show all food items clearly
- ✅ Include reference objects (fork, phone) for portion estimation
- ✅ Capture from directly above when possible
- ❌ Avoid blurry or dark photos
- ❌ Don't hide food items behind others

## 🔥 2. Build Your Streak

### What is a Streak?
A streak is the number of consecutive days you've logged at least one meal. The longer your streak, the more features you unlock!

### How to Maintain Your Streak:
1. Log **at least one meal per day**
2. Check your streak on the **Dashboard** (top card with flame icon)
3. Use **Streak Shields** to protect against missed days (earned at milestones)

### Streak Milestones:
- **7 days** → Unlock Custom Themes 🎨
- **30 days** → Unlock Streak Calendar 📅
- **60 days** → Unlock Nutrition Insights & CSV Export 📊
- **100 days** → Earn 1 Month Free Pro Access 🏆
- **180 days** → Earn 2 Months Free Pro Access ⭐
- **365 days** → Earn 6 Months Free Pro Access 👑

---

## 🛡️ 3. Streak Shields Explained

### What are Shields?
Shields protect your streak if you miss a day. They're automatically consumed when needed.

### How to Earn Shields:
- **1 shield** at 7-day streak
- **2 shields** at 30-day streak
- **3 shields** (max) at 100-day streak

### How Shields Work:
- You can have a maximum of **3 shields**
- If you miss a day, **1 shield is consumed** automatically
- Your streak continues unbroken
- If you have **0 shields** and miss a day, your streak resets to 0

### Check Your Shields:
- View shield count on **Dashboard** (Streak card)
- See shield usage history in **Settings** → Streak Calendar

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How accurate is the AI analysis?
**A:** Our AI uses Gemini 2.0 Flash for "scarily accurate" nutrition analysis. It considers portion sizes, food density, and visual specifics. Accuracy improves with better photo quality.

### Q: What happens if I miss a day?
**A:** If you have shields, one is consumed automatically and your streak continues. If you have no shields, your streak resets to 0.

### Q: Can I edit meal entries?
**A:** Currently, meal entries are final after saving. Future updates will add editing capabilities.

### Q: How do I earn more shields?
**A:** Shields are earned automatically at streak milestones: 1 at 7 days, 2 at 30 days, 3 (max) at 100 days.

### Q: When do features unlock?
**A:** Features unlock automatically when you reach the required streak days. Check Settings to see your progress.

### Q: Is my data private?
**A:** Yes! Your data is stored securely in Supabase with row-level security. Only you can access your meal entries.

### Q: Does CalorieWise cost money?
**A:** The app is completely free! All core features are available at no cost. Pro features can be unlocked through streaks (also free).

### Q: Can I use CalorieWise offline?
**A:** You need internet for photo analysis (AI processing). Viewing past data works offline.
